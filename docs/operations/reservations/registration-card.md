# Registration Card

The **Registration Card** is an editable, signed check-in record stored against the reservation — distinct from the printable [Registration Form](/operations/reservations/registration-form). Where the Registration Form is a document you print or email, the Registration Card is an on-screen form where staff can adjust the guest's details and capture the guest's (and an accompanying guest's) signature digitally before printing.

:::tip **Having a problem?**
See [Reservations Overview](/operations/reservations/overview) for common FAQs and troubleshooting.
:::

## Registration Form vs. Registration Card

| | Registration Form | Registration Card |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Printable/emailable check-in document. | Editable, signed record kept on the reservation. |
| Editing | Fields come from the reservation/guest profile as printed. | Individual fields (name, dates, room, rate, address, etc.) can be corrected in the editor before saving. |
| Signature | Printed signature line only (signed on paper). | Signature captured digitally on-screen and stored with the reservation. |
| Storage | Not stored — generated fresh each time it's printed. | Saved on the reservation; can be reopened and reviewed later. |

## Access

From the reservation detail, click **Registration Card**. The editor opens pre-filled from the reservation and guest profile (arrival/departure dates, room, rate, guest name, contact details, nationality, etc.).

## Editing and Capturing Signatures

1. Open the reservation and click **Registration Card**.
2. Review and correct the pre-filled fields if needed.
3. Ask the guest to sign in the **Guest Signature** box using a mouse, stylus, or touchscreen. There is also an **Accompanying Guest Signature** box for a second signer if applicable.
4. Optionally set the signing date for each signature.
5. Click **Save** to store the card and signatures against the reservation.

The signature pad captures a hand-drawn signature directly on screen; use **Clear**/the delete action on the pad to redo a signature before saving.

## Printing or Emailing

From the Registration Card editor you can:
- **Preview** the card as a PDF before saving.
- **Print** the card.
- **Email** it to the guest — the recipient address is pre-filled from the guest profile email and can be edited before sending.

## When to Use

- At check-in, once the guest has reviewed their details, to capture a legally usable digital signature.
- To keep a permanent, retrievable signed record for the reservation instead of (or alongside) a paper form.
