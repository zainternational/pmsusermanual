# Reservation Groups

A **Reservation Group** links multiple reservations together — for example, rooms booked for a wedding party, a conference, or a tour group. Grouped reservations share a group ID and group name, and each member has a sequence number within the group.

:::tip **Having a problem?**
See [Reservations Overview](/operations/reservations/overview) for common FAQs and troubleshooting.
:::

## Access

From the sidebar: **Reservation → Groups** (also reachable from the group management option on the All Reservations list).

## Group List

The group list shows every reservation group with its group ID (formatted as a 6-digit number), group name, and creation date. Open a group to see its member reservations.

## Creating a Group

1. Go to **Reservation → Groups** and click **New Group** (or add a group directly while creating/editing a reservation).
2. Enter a **Group Name**.
3. Save. The group is created with an auto-generated group ID.

A group is also created automatically the first time a reservation is saved with a new group name — you do not have to create the group first.

## Adding Reservations to a Group

When creating or editing a reservation, assign it to an existing group (or create a new one by name). The reservation becomes a group member and receives the next sequence number in that group.

## Editing a Group

Open the group from the list and click **Edit** to change the group name. Save to apply the change — it updates the name shown on every member reservation.

## Deleting a Group

Open the group and click **Delete**. Deleting a group does not delete its member reservations; it removes the group record itself, so make sure member reservations no longer need to be grouped (or have been reassigned) before deleting.

## Group Billing Note

The first reservation created in a group holds the deposit for the group; other members of the same group do not require a separate deposit.
